# Business Positioning

The project supports refined marketplace operations aimed at driving product transactions. Its scope covers:

- **Product expressiveness**: Improve product information and presentation (images, videos, cases, qualifications, versions).
- **Product transactionability**: Improve conversion-oriented content and offers.
- **Product compliance**: Review products against marketplace and legal requirements.
- **Product status management**: Track and maintain listing states.
- **Product pricing management**: Track and maintain prices.
- **Marketing campaigns**: Plan and run promotions.
- **Platform affairs**: Advance marketplace-side processes and communications.

Any new spec, issue template, or skill should map to one or more of these areas and avoid duplicating existing ones.
